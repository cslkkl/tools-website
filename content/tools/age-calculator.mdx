## What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator computes the exact time difference between two dates — typically a birth date and today's date (or any other reference date). It breaks down the result into years, months, and days, and also shows the total number of days.

## Why Use Our Age Calculator?

- **Exact breakdown** — Years, months, days, and total days
- **Custom date ranges** — Calculate age at any reference date
- **Private** — Your dates stay in your browser
