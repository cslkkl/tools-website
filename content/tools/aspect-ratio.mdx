## What is an Aspect Ratio Calculator?

An aspect ratio describes the proportional relationship between width and height. Common examples include 16:9 (HD video), 4:3 (traditional TV), and 1:1 (Instagram photos). Our calculator computes the simplified ratio from any width/height and shows you which standard ratio it matches.
