## What is a Discount Calculator?

A discount calculator computes the final price of an item after applying a percentage discount. Enter the original price and the discount percentage, and it instantly shows how much you save and the final sale price.

## Why Use Our Discount Calculator?

- **Simple two-input design** — Just the original price and discount percentage
- **Shows savings** — See both the amount saved and the final price

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I calculate multi-layer discounts?
Calculate them sequentially: apply the first discount, then use the resulting price as the "original price" for the second discount.
