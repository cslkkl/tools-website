## What is a Stopwatch & Timer?

A stopwatch tracks elapsed time with precision, and a countdown timer alerts you when a set duration has passed. Our tool combines both in one clean interface — use the stopwatch with lap recording for workouts and meetings, or the countdown timer for cooking, presentations, and focused work sessions.

## Why Use Our Stopwatch & Timer?

- **Dual mode** — Stopwatch with lap recording and countdown timer in one tool
- **Precise** — Updates every 50ms for smooth display
- **Works in background** — Timer continues even if you switch tabs

## How to Use

### Stopwatch mode
1. Click **Start** to begin tracking
2. Click **Lap** to record split times
3. Click **Stop** when done
4. Click **Reset** to clear

### Timer mode
1. Switch to **Countdown**
2. Set the timer duration in seconds
3. Click **Start**
4. Timer counts down to zero
