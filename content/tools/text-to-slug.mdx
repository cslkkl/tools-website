## What is a Text to Slug Converter?

A slug is the URL-friendly version of a title or phrase — lowercase, with spaces replaced by hyphens and special characters removed. For example, "How to Cook Pasta!" becomes `how-to-cook-pasta`. Our tool converts any text to a clean slug instantly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I use hyphens or underscores in URLs?
Hyphens. Google treats hyphens as word separators but treats underscores as word joiners. `my-blog-post` is better for SEO than `my_blog_post`.
