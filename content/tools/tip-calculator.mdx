## What is a Tip Calculator?

A tip calculator helps you figure out how much to tip at restaurants, bars, and other services. Enter the bill amount, choose a tip percentage (or select from common presets), and specify the number of people splitting the bill. The calculator shows the tip amount, total bill, and per-person cost.

## Why Use Our Tip Calculator?

- **Preset percentages** — Quick-select 10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, or 25%
- **Bill splitting** — Enter the number of people to split the check
- **Instant results** — Tip, total, and per-person amounts calculated immediately

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's the standard tip percentage?
In the US, 15% is considered standard for adequate service, 18-20% for good to excellent service.
